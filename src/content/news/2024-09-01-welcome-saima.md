We are delighted to welcome [Saima Afrin](/people/saima-afrin/) to the AURA Lab. Starting in September 2024, Saima begins her Ph.D. under the mentorship of Prof. Mastropaolo, working on **sustainable, resource-efficient large language models for code**.

Saima comes to William & Mary from Daffodil International University in Bangladesh, where she earned her B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering in 2019 and later returned as a full-time lecturer in the same department. She arrives with teaching experience as well as research training — a combination that shows in how she frames a problem.

Her work here sits on the **Green AI** side of the lab: quantization, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and resource-efficient code summarization. The premise is that the models now automating software engineering tasks are growing faster than the budgets — computational, financial and environmental — available to run them, and that the interesting question is how much of their capability survives being made affordable.

That question turns out to have a longer answer than expected, and Saima has spent her first years here supplying it: whether QLoRA can fine-tune a code model for summarization at a fraction of the parameters, whether quantization damages the quality of the code a model writes, and what the literature on parameter-efficient fine-tuning actually establishes. Welcome, Saima.
