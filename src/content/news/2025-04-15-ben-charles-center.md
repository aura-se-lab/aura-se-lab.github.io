Congratulations to [Ben Tremblay](/people/benjamin-tremblay/) on receiving W&M's *Charles Center Summer Research Grant*! Ben's research explores the intersection of software engineering, AI, and optimization, with a focus on cutting the cost of training the large language models that power development automation (e.g., GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT) — addressing the urgent need for resource-efficient scaling as models grow to trillions of parameters.
