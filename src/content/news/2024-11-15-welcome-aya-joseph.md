We are thrilled to welcome [Aya Garryyeva](/people/aya-garryyeva/) and [Joseph Call](/people/joseph-call/) to the AURA Lab. Both begin their Ph.D. research in January 2025, and between them they cover two of the four directions the lab works in.

**Aya** works at the intersection of AI and software engineering with Prof. Mastropaolo and [Denys Poshyvanyk](https://www.cs.wm.edu/~denys/), on making models for software engineering tasks **interpretable and trustworthy**. She comes to the Ph.D. having worked as a data scientist, with a B.S. in Mathematics from Wingate University and an M.S. in Computer Science from William & Mary — so she has seen what these models look like from outside the research literature as well as inside it. She is active in STEM outreach and welcomes questions from undergraduate and master's students considering research.

**Joseph** works on **resource-efficient machine learning** for code analysis and generation: how to make these models cheaper to adapt and serve without giving up what makes them useful. Originally from La Paz, Honduras, he plays guitar and cooks when he is not benchmarking something.

Both start on work that was already waiting for them — Aya on neurosymbolic program comprehension, Joseph on resource-efficient code summarization — and both had their names on papers within their first months here. Welcome to the lab.
