Our work *From Triumph to Uncertainty: The Journey of Software Engineering in the AI Era* has been accepted for publication in **ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology (TOSEM)**. The paper, with Camilo Escobar-Velásquez and Mario Linares-Vásquez, outlines the elements we believe are vital for integrating AI into software engineering while preserving the human creativity that drives the field.
