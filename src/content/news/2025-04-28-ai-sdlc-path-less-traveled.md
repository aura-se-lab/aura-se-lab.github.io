*A Path Less Traveled: Reimagining Software Engineering Automation via a Neurosymbolic Paradigm*, co-authored with Denys Poshyvanyk, has been accepted at the [AI-SDLC 2025](https://conf.researchr.org/home/fse-2025/ai-sdlc-2025) workshop, co-located with the ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering (FSE'25) in Trondheim, Norway.
