The National Science Foundation has funded our CRII proposal *SHF: Advancing Sustainable Software Engineering Practices with Energy-Efficient Large Language Models for Code* ([award #2451058](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2451058)). PI: Antonio Mastropaolo; June 1, 2025 – May 31, 2027. The award supports research on reducing the compute, energy, and carbon cost of training and deploying LLMs for code through task-aware training-data optimization and model compression.
