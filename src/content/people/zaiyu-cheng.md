Zaiyu Cheng is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at William & Mary. His research sits at the intersection of AI and Software Engineering, with a focus on using large language models for code tasks — studying how data integration, pre-training, and fine-tuning can boost model performance while reducing costs.

He holds a B.Sc. from the University of Birmingham (2022) and an M.Sc. from the University of Manchester (2024); four years in the UK shaped both his academic path and personal outlook. Outside research he enjoys video games, running, and swimming.
