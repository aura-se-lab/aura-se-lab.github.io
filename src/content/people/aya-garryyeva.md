Aya is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at William & Mary, working with Dr. Antonio Mastropaolo and [Dr. Denys Poshyvanyk](https://www.cs.wm.edu/~denys/) at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering. Her primary research goal is to make AI models for software engineering tasks more interpretable and trustworthy.

Prior to her Ph.D., she worked as a Data Scientist and earned a B.S. in Mathematics from Wingate University and an M.S. in Computer Science from William & Mary. She is passionate about STEM outreach and welcomes questions from undergraduate and master's students about pursuing research, and is open to collaborations in her area of interest.

Outside of research, Aya enjoys travel and educational documentaries, historical fiction, yoga and Pilates, hiking, and listening to people's stories.
