Saima Afrin is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at William & Mary, specializing in applying Artificial Intelligence to automate Software Engineering, with a strong emphasis on Green AI. Since joining the AURA Lab in the Fall of 2024, she has focused on Green AI techniques to optimize Large Language Models of Code (LLMc): quantization, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and resource-efficient code summarization.

Before her Ph.D., Saima earned a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University, Bangladesh (2019), where she later served as a full-time lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, supporting research and education. Her vision is to integrate sustainable AI solutions into software engineering and beyond.
