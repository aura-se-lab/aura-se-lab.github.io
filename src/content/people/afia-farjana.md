Afia is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at William & Mary, working with Dr. Antonio Mastropaolo at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering, focusing on prompt engineering and Large Language Models. Her research aims to design intelligent prompting strategies that make software engineering tasks — such as code understanding and generation — more accurate and effective.

She earned her bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the American International University–Bangladesh (AIUB) and worked as a Software Quality Assurance Engineer at DataBiz Software Limited, gaining hands-on experience in software testing, automation, and system reliability. During her master's in Computer Science at the University of South Dakota she worked in the Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence Lab, which inspired her to explore how AI can enhance software engineering practice.

Outside of research, Afia enjoys cooking, exploring new places, and photography.
