Ben is an undergraduate Computer Science student at William & Mary specializing in AI/ML. Under the mentorship of Dr. Antonio Mastropaolo, he researches the intersection of software engineering and AI optimization, with a particular focus on *large code models*.

His work explores parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) and neural compression techniques — including quantization, pruning, and adapter methods like LoRA/QLoRA — applied to code generation models. The central question driving his research: **when optimization methods alter a model's underlying topology, what happens to code quality beyond mere correctness?** While functional correctness might be preserved, he investigates whether maintainability, security, readability, and adherence to best practices degrade under these structural transformations, with the goal of evaluation frameworks and optimization strategies that achieve computational efficiency without sacrificing the holistic quality of generated code.
