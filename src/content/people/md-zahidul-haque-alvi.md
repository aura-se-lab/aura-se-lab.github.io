Alvi is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at William & Mary, working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering with a focus on Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning techniques for large code models. His research explores how to make these models more adaptable and efficient for real-world software engineering tasks.

Before starting his Ph.D., he completed a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Sylhet Engineering College in Bangladesh. His interest in AI began during his final year through deep learning projects in NLP. He has worked as a Junior Machine Learning Engineer at Maya and as a Junior Software Engineer at Graaho, gaining hands-on experience with real-world AI systems.

Outside research, Alvi stays active and makes time for the gym every day — and is always happy to chat about AI, software engineering, or anything in between.
