AURALAB.SH · PUBLICATIONS · VENUE BADGE · ROUND THREE

The seal, made honest

Six papers now, four of them peer-reviewed and two preprints — because the badge should say which. A struck seal means somebody certified it; an open, broken-ring seal means nobody has yet. That is the one distinction the grid was not making, and it is the distinction this lab cares about most.

E1

Seal · struck

The refined seal. A milled coin edge, a second inner ring for CORE A*, stars for the tier, and a light source at the top left so it reads as pressed rather than drawn. A preprint gets a different seal, not the same one — open ring, broken border, PREPRINT · NOT PEER-REVIEWED. The badge now states what it is instead of decorating it.
