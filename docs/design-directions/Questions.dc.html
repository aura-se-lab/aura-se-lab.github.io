THE QUESTIONS EACH ONE LINKS TO THE PAPER THAT ANSWERS IT, OR TO NOTHING YET

Is quantization a deal-breaker for code models?

No. Under activation-aware quantization, CodeLlama and DeepSeek-Coder kept passing tests — and kept the maintainability and structural simplicity developers look for.

S. AFRIN · B. XU · A. MASTROPAOLO — ICSME 2025 · 13 CITATIONS

EFFICIENCY

Is code-comment coherence enough to clean a training set?

Not on its own. Halving the training data barely moved summarization quality — and selecting by coherence did no better than selecting at random. Today's datasets are mostly filler; the right quality attribute is still open.

Can one language model be trusted to judge another's code?

Our most-cited study to date puts LLM-as-a-judge under scrutiny for code generation and summarization — where its verdicts track human ones, and where they do not.

EFFICIENCY

Can a code model explain itself at the level a developer thinks?

Token-level attributions are not how engineers reason. We are after feature-level explanations, and a neurosymbolic layer that can be checked rather than trusted. First results are in; the question is not closed.

INTERPRETABILITY

What does an agent that ships software actually have to be measured on?

Snippet benchmarks do not tell you whether an agent can carry an issue to a reviewed change. We are building the end-to-end measurements — and expect them to be unflattering.
