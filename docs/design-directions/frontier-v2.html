DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE · WILLIAM & MARY

Efficient, interpretable and rigorously evaluated AI for software engineering.

The AURA Lab, founded in Fall 2024, works on the cost, transparency and evaluation of AI for software engineering: making code models cheap enough to deploy, legible enough to trust, and measured by methods that hold up. Directed by Antonio Mastropaolo.
